About us

You start the engine, your trip starts properly

Jatta Cars is a family-run hire company working out of Berlin. We are small enough that the person who answers the phone is the person who hands you the keys, and stubborn enough to keep the pricing honest while the big chains take the other approach.

Every car is bought new or nearly new, kept on a strict service schedule and retired before it can become someone's bad day.
